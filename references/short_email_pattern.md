# Short Outreach Email Pattern

Use this reference for the default Chinese or English professor outreach email. Treat bracketed text as drafting guidance; never leave placeholders in the final message.

## Core formula

`one target + one hook + one or two applicant proofs + one honest bridge + one ask`

The CV carries detailed courses, awards, methods, internships, and project lists. Include those details in the email only when they strengthen the target-specific case.

## Chinese structure

1. **Salutation and purpose:** Address the professor correctly, thank them briefly, and state the target opportunity and cycle.
2. **Applicant identity:** Give the applicant's name, current affiliation/status, and expected graduation or career stage when relevant.
3. **Applicant proof:** State one primary experience with the applicant's actual role, work, result, and exact status. Add at most one distinct secondary proof when it improves the match.
4. **Professor direction and hook:** Name up to three verified active directions, then exactly one paper or active project. Explain one mechanism, design choice, or finding that drew the applicant's attention.
5. **Fit bridge:** Explain the match with evidence. For adjacent or exploratory fit, name the difference in research setting, the transferable capability, and one concrete target-side question.
6. **Attachment and ask:** Mention only confirmed attachments. Ask one low-friction question about the opportunity or a short conversation.
7. **Closing and signature:** Use a restrained wishes line and only the applicant's requested name, affiliation, contact details, and date.

Adapt the order naturally when the sentence flow requires it. Avoid forcing a paragraph for every CV category. Use a concise, professional, first-person voice like this pattern:

```text
尊敬的[教授姓名]老师：

感谢您在百忙之中阅读来信。我是[学校/院系/专业/年级]的[姓名]，预计[毕业时间]毕业，希望申请[目标周期与身份]。

我曾[申请人实际负责的工作及结果，保留准确状态]。这段经历让我持续关注[相关问题或能力]。

了解到您团队围绕[不超过三个当前研究方向]开展研究后，我关注到[唯一论文/项目]。[用一两句说明其具体问题、机制或发现]。我的经历与这一方向[直接相关/属于相邻场景/目前主要是探索兴趣]；其中[可迁移的能力或证据]可以作为我继续探索[目标侧具体问题]的基础。

[如已确认附件，简短说明。]冒昧请教您是否考虑[目标周期/身份]申请，或是否方便进一步交流[研究匹配/申请安排]？

祝工作顺利！
[姓名]
[所属单位]
[仅列用户要求的联系方式]
```

The pattern is a guide to sentence functions, not mandatory wording. Preserve the user's preferred wording where possible and change only what the target evidence or grammar requires.

## English structure

Use the same evidence order in natural English: salutation and purpose; current status and strongest proof; one specific target-side hook; honest fit bridge; confirmed attachment and low-friction ask; professional sign-off.

## Writing rules

- Make each sentence perform one job; remove repetition and generic praise.
- Prefer actual roles, methods, outcomes, and exact project/publication status over broad claims.
- Do not summarize several professor papers or copy an abstract.
- Name the applicant's work clearly after discussing the professor's work; avoid ambiguous references such as “this project” when multiple projects are mentioned.
- Do not claim an attachment merely because it is commonly expected.
- Keep URLs, citations, internal fit labels, and research notes outside the email body.
- Recheck names, dates, metrics, status labels, contact details, and recruiting claims before finalizing.
- Remove every placeholder and zero-width character.
